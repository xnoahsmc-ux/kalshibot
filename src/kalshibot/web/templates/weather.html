How this helps the bot

For any Kalshi weather market (e.g. KXHIGHLAX-26APR22-T72 "Will LA high be above 72°F?"), we pull the NWS forecast high for that city and compute P(actual > 72°F) using a 3°F standard deviation. That probability is blended with the technical ensemble's view, giving significantly more accurate calls on weather markets than pure price action alone.
