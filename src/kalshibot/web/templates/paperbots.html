How this works

Each bot watches the live signal stream. When a signal matches its confidence and edge thresholds, it "opens" a simulated position at the current ask/bid price. All positions are marked to market every poll. When a market is about to close (< 5 min to close) the bot settles at the last known mid and the realized PnL is added to its cash.

Tips
  • Make sure the live feed is running - without it no signals reach the bots.
  • The bots only trade tickers where the recommendation isn't "flat".
  • Whichever bot style is winning by end of day is the profile to use live.
