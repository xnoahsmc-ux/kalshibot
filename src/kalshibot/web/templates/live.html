What these mean
plain-English glossary
  • Market price — what Kalshi is charging to bet YES on this market right now (in cents).
  • Bot's estimate — the probability the ensemble thinks YES will actually win.
  • Stake — how much the bot would bet, sized via half-Kelly capped at your per-market limit.
  • Expected profit — average dollar payoff on this stake if you repeated it many times.
  • Confidence: HIGH — edge > 10% and all strategies agree. Strongest signal.
  • Confidence: MED — edge > 5%, strategies mostly agree.
  • Confidence: LOW — thin edge or strategies disagree. Skip these.
How to trade for real

This page is read-only by default. To place the suggested bets automatically, stop the web UI, edit .env and set KALSHIBOT_DRY_RUN=false, then restart. Start with a tiny KALSHIBOT_MAX_POSITION_USD (like $5) and a high KALSHIBOT_MIN_EDGE (like 0.08) to only take high-confidence plays.
