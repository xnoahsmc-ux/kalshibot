How picks are chosen
  1. Live signals are scored with the ensemble + real-data fair value (NWS/ESPN).
  2. For each potential bet we compute EV per dollar: p_win × (1−price)/price − (1−p_win).
  3. Singles are the top EV-positive picks, sorted by expected profit at the suggested stake.
  4. Combos take 2–3 picks from different genres and series, multiply their independent win probabilities, and only show combos that are still EV-positive.
