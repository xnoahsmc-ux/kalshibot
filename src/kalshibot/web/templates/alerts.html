Setup
Two ways to receive SMS

Option A — Twilio (recommended): sign up at twilio.com, grab a free trial number ($15 credit), add to .env: 

KALSHIBOT_PHONE=4087637531
KALSHIBOT_TWILIO_SID=ACxxxxxxxxxxxx
KALSHIBOT_TWILIO_TOKEN=xxxxxxxxxxxx
KALSHIBOT_TWILIO_FROM=+1xxxxxxxxxx

Option B — Email-to-SMS gateway (free): figure out your carrier and use their email gateway. Edit .env: 

KALSHIBOT_NOTIFY_EMAIL=4087637531@vtext.com   # Verizon
# AT&T = @txt.att.net    | T-Mobile = @tmomail.net
# Sprint = @messaging.sprintpcs.com
KALSHIBOT_SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
KALSHIBOT_SMTP_PORT=587
KALSHIBOT_SMTP_USER=you@gmail.com
KALSHIBOT_SMTP_PASS=app-specific-password

Restart the server after editing .env.
